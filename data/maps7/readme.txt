Gold Mine, LearnToPlay, Sunny Side Up, Tsunami and Tutorial are created using
the map_convert_07 tool for server compatibility with Teeworlds 0.7 clients.

ctf1, ctf2, ctf3, ctf4, ctf6, ctf7, dm1, dm2, dm6, dm8 and dm9 are the maps as
Teeworlds 0.7.5 ships them (CC-BY-SA 3.0, like the rest of its data). Their
game layers are those of the maps of the same names in maps/. ctf5 and dm7 are
missing because Teeworlds 0.7 changed their game layers.
